# 🎨 Freepik & AI Prompts — Fitness Aesthetics

Guia de prompts para manter a consistência visual premium em campanhas e mocks.

---

## 1. Estilo Visual Medicinal Pharma (The Prompt DNA)
- **Lighting:** Cinematic, dramatic rim lighting, moody gym environment.
- **Texture:** Hyper-realistic, 8k, detailed textures (powder splashes, sweat droplets).
- **Colors:** Dominant deep blues, metallic accents, hits of crimson red.
- **Mood:** Professional, elite, hardcore yet sophisticated.

---

## 2. Prompts Curados

### A. Showcases de Produto (Splash & Texture)
> **Prompt:** `Professional product photography of a premium black supplement tub, dynamic water and milk splash around the bottle, high-speed photography, deep blue and gold lighting, hyper-realistic, 8k resolution, cinematic studio lighting --ar 4:5`

### B. Backgrounds para Carrosséis (The Lab)
> **Prompt:** `Modern minimalist laboratory interior, blurred fitness equipment in the background, neon blue accents, high-tech science vibe, professional photography, clean composition --ar 16:9`

### C. Lifestyle / Atletas (Elite Performance)
> **Prompt:** `Close-up of a muscular athlete in a dark moody gym, dramatic side lighting, sweat on skin, wearing high-end navy blue athletic gear, focus on intensity and determination, cinematic look, 8k --ar 9:16`

### D. Texturas de Ingredientes
> **Prompt:** `Macro photography of pure white micronized creatine powder, crystalline texture, sharp focus, laboratory aesthetic, soft blue lighting, abstract composition --ar 1:1`

---

## 3. Comandos Negativos (Negative Prompts)
A serem usados para filtrar resultados indesejados:
`cartoon, 3d render (unless specified), low quality, blurry, cheap gym, bright colors, cluttered background, generic models, smiling faces (keep it serious/intense).`

---

## 4. Dicas de Otimização (Freepik AI)
- Use a opção **"Realism"** no seletor de estilo.
- Adicione as palavras **"Gourmet food style"** para fotos de Whey e sabores.
- Para texturas de pó, use **"High-speed macro photography"**.
